About

From the field to the frameworks

I spent eight years in the Army as a Paratrooper and a Special Forces Operator before spending the last fourteen building Android software — and the two careers have more in common than you'd think. Both reward precision, calm under pressure, and getting the fundamentals right before anything else.

I started as a hobbyist contributing to the AOKP custom ROM community, and I've since shipped Android for automotive OEMs, secure enterprise apps, and consumer products used by millions — usually as the person leading the team, not just writing the code. Lately that means modernizing legacy codebases into Jetpack Compose and Kotlin, and building cross-platform apps with Kotlin Multiplatform.
